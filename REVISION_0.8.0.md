# James AI v0.8.0 — Verified Student Community

## Added
- School-ID verification with admin approval/rejection and green verified tick.
- Private school-ID storage with secure admin-only review endpoint.
- School-ID images are deleted after the review decision to reduce retention.
- Study-first social feed for school-verified students.
- Up to five JPG/JPEG photos per post; browser converts non-JPG image selections to JPG before upload.
- Likes, views, comments, shares and sharing into existing study rooms.
- Post reporting connected to the admin safety queue.

## Community rules
- Posting, liking, commenting and sharing require approved school verification.
- The feed never exposes the school-ID document path.
- The green tick means James AI approved the student's submitted school ID; it is not institutional endorsement.

## Privacy
School-ID images are private and removed after an admin decision. This design follows a data-minimisation approach; retention, access controls, privacy notice and institutional responsibilities must still be finalized before production launch.
