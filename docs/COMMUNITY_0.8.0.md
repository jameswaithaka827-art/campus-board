# Verified student community

The green badge means a James AI administrator approved the user's school-ID verification. It does not mean institutional endorsement.

Students can publish notes, projects, questions, resources and achievements. The feed supports reactions, comments, view counts, shares and sharing into study rooms.

Images are limited to JPG/JPEG and five per post. Browser conversion makes common phone image formats upload as JPG; the server still enforces JPEG and size limits. Community images are stored privately and served through an authenticated proxy.

School-ID documents are treated as private verification evidence. They are accessible only to an authorized admin review route and are deleted after the decision. Before production use, finalize the privacy notice, retention schedule, institution/controller responsibilities and legal basis with the relevant organization. Kenya's Data Protection Act requires data minimisation and retention only as long as necessary, and the ODPC education guidance explicitly covers e-learning services.
